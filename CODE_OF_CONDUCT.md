# Code of Conduct

HayaSend contributors must keep project spaces professional, welcoming, and
focused on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private
information, and sustained personal attacks are not accepted. Technical
disagreement is welcome when it is specific, evidence-based, and respectful.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through
the organization owners rather than through a public issue.
